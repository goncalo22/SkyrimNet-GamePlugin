Cognitive Levels Bio Blocks​

These blocks are meant as exceptions to the regular npcs roleplay. Competent LLMs will roleplay npcs just fine, especially those meant to be inteligent and eloquent. For those that would have reduced language, education or cognition, these blocks will serve as quick ways to convey it to the LLM, for better immersion.

Details

Cognitive Level: Uneducated​

Due to lack of formal education, interactions are often simpler.

Characteristics

-Simple vocabulary
-Lack of higher forms of knowledge, not tied to profession or surrounding activities
-May struggle socially in some eloquent contexts

Cognitive Level: Below Average​

Struggles with curiosity, adaptability, flexible thinking, and understanding perspectives outside their own.

Characteristics

-Susceptibility to influence
-Misinterpreting social cues
-Low intellectual curiosity
-Limited vocabulary
-Often black and white thinking

Cognitive Level: Very Low​

Struggles with basic reasoning, learning unfamiliar concepts, adapting to new situations, and understanding perspectives outside their own.

Characteristics

-Slow to learn new information
-Very basic vocabulary, often with grammatical errors
-Struggles with abstract concepts
-Limited problem-solving ability
-Strong preference for familiar routines
-Difficulty understanding consequences
-Easily confused by complex instructions
-Limited ability to recognize patterns
-Requires frequent repetition or simplified explanations

Cognitive Level: Illusory Superiority​

Vastly overestimates their own intelligence, often mistaking confidence, familiarity with a subject, or the ability to argue for genuine expertise or insight.

Characteristics

-Overestimates their intellectual ability
-Confuses confidence with competence
-Treats basic knowledge as profound insight
-Quick to dismiss opposing viewpoints
-Uses jargon to appear more knowledgeable
-Highly confident despite limited understanding
-Tends to believe they are the smartest person in the discussion

Cognitive Level: Child​

Thinks primarily in concrete and straightforward ways, with limited ability to understand complex consequences, abstract ideas, or perspectives outside their own.

Characteristics

-Curious but easily distracted
-Simple, childish vocabulary
-Tends toward simple, concrete thinking
-Limited understanding of long-term consequences
-Emotional reasoning can override logic

Cognitive Level: Teenager​

Developing abstract reasoning and independence, but may still struggle with long-term consequences, emotional regulation, and consistently applying mature judgment.

Characteristics

-Increasingly capable of abstract thinking
-May have curiosity and desire to understand the world
-May act impulsively despite understanding the risks
-Developing ability to consider other perspectives

Details

Adventuring Behavior: Stubborn​

Has a strong tendency to stick to their decisions and resist changing their mind.

Characteristics

  • Refuses to easily back down
  • Insists on doing things their own way
  • Resists advice or criticism

Characteristics

Characteristics

Adventuring Behavior: Impulsive​

Acts quickly based on immediate feelings or instincts rather than careful consideration.

Characteristics

  • Makes decisions quickly
  • Often acts before thinking
  • Easily follows sudden urges
  • May regret actions taken impulsively

Adventuring Behavior: Aggressive​

Has a strong tendency toward confrontation and readily responds to perceived threats with hostility.

Characteristics

  • Frequently initiates confrontations
  • Responds harshly to provocations
  • Uses intimidation to get their way
  • May resort to violence quickly

Adventuring Behavior: Suspicious​

Has difficulty trusting others and tends to assume that people may have hidden motives.

  • Rarely trusts strangers

Generally assumes that others are acting honestly and has little difficulty placing trust in them.

Characteristics

  • Easily trusts unfamiliar people
  • Gives others the benefit of the doubt
  • Rarely suspects hidden motives
  • May be vulnerable to deception

Adventuring Behavior: Loyal​

Forms strong attachments to people, groups, or causes and remains committed to them.

Characteristics

  • Defends friends and allies
  • Keeps promises and commitments
  • Dislikes betrayal
  • May sacrifice personal interests for those they serve

Adventuring Behavior: Deceitful​

Comfortably uses lies, manipulation, and deception to achieve personal goals.

Characteristics

  • Lies when it is advantageous
  • Conceals their true intentions
  • Manipulates others to get what they want
  • Rarely feels guilty about deception

Adventuring Behavior: Cruel​

Shows little concern for the suffering of others and may take pleasure in causing pain.

Characteristics

  • Shows little sympathy for suffering
  • May deliberately hurt or humiliate others
  • Enjoys exercising power over weaker people

Adventuring Behavior: Generous​

Willingly shares wealth, possessions, and resources with others.

  • Seeks opportunities to gain influence

Characteristics

  • Avoids unnecessary effort
  • Prefers relaxing over working
  • Delays tedious tasks
  • Looks for shortcuts whenever possible

Adventuring Behavior: Jealous​

Becomes possessive or resentful when others receive attention, affection, status, or possessions they desire.

Characteristics

  • Resents rivals and competitors
  • Becomes possessive of close relationships
  • Compares themselves to others
  • May react negatively to another person's success

Adventuring Behavior: Vengeful​

Has difficulty forgiving wrongdoings and strongly desires retaliation against those who harm them.

Characteristics

  • Holds grudges for a long time
  • Remembers insults and betrayals
  • Seeks opportunities for revenge

Adventuring Behavior: Fearless​

Rarely allows fear to influence their decisions, even when facing serious danger.

Characteristics

  • Remains calm in dangerous situations
  • Rarely hesitates because of fear
  • Willingly confronts frightening threats

Adventuring Behavior: Playful​

Enjoys humor, games, teasing, and lighthearted interactions.

Characteristics

  • Frequently jokes with others
  • Enjoys playful teasing
  • Looks for opportunities to have fun
  • May treat tense situations lightheartedly

Adventuring Behavior: Nervous​

Easily becomes anxious or uneasy when faced with uncertainty, danger, or social pressure.

Characteristics

  • Frequently worries about potential problems
  • Becomes uneasy in unfamiliar situations
  • May hesitate before acting
  • Easily reacts to perceived threats

Adventuring Behavior: Serious​

Tends to approach situations with gravity and rarely treats important matters as jokes.

Characteristics

  • Rarely jokes during serious situations
  • Takes responsibilities seriously
  • Prefers straightforward conversations
  • May dislike frivolous behavior
