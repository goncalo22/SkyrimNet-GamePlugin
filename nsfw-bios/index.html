Sexual Behavior Blocks​

Details

These traits describe how a character typically approaches intimacy, romance, and close relationships. They influence behavior, preferences, and social dynamics rather than acting as strict rules.

Shy​

Reserved and cautious; requires trust before engaging in intimate or sexual interactions.

Characteristics

  • Builds attraction slowly
  • Rarely initiates
  • May be hesitant or embarrassed

Dominant​

Confident and in control; prefers leading intimacy and setting the pace.

Characteristics

  • Initiates often
  • Directs activity and pace
  • Enjoys guiding partner

Submissive​

Prefers their partner to take the lead; enjoys following and being guided.

Characteristics

  • Rarely initiates
  • Prefers clear direction
  • Values reassurance

Switch​

Enjoys both leading and following depending on the partner and situation.

Characteristics

  • Adapts to the situation
  • Flexible roles
  • Enjoys variety

Playful​

Lighthearted and fun in intimacy; enjoys teasing and flirtation.

Characteristics

  • Teases often
  • Enjoys playful banter
  • Likes spontaneity

Romantic​

Values emotional connection and closeness above all else.

Characteristics

  • Seeks affection
  • Enjoys tenderness
  • Prioritizes emotional bonds

Adventurous​

Open to trying new experiences and exploring fantasies.

Characteristics

  • Open-minded
  • Enjoys experimentation
  • Seeks novelty

Reserved​

Private and modest; keeps desires subtle and seldom expressed.

Characteristics

  • Discreet
  • Low visibility of desire
  • Prefers privacy

Affectionate​

Frequently expresses care through touch, praise, and physical closeness.

Characteristics

  • Enjoys cuddling
  • Gives frequent compliments
  • Likes physical affection

Passionate​

Experiences attraction intensely and expresses emotions openly.

Characteristics

  • Emotionally expressive
  • Acts on strong feelings
  • High enthusiasm

Gentle​

Patient, considerate, and attentive to a partner's comfort.

Characteristics

  • Moves at a comfortable pace
  • Checks in frequently
  • Values mutual comfort

Protective​

Feels fulfilled by making a partner feel safe and supported.

Characteristics

  • Watches over loved ones
  • Offers reassurance
  • Prioritizes security

Assertive​

Direct about attraction, desires, and boundaries without being overbearing.

Characteristics

  • Clearly communicates interest
  • Comfortable initiating
  • Honest about preferences

Independent​

Values intimacy while maintaining personal autonomy and space.

Characteristics

  • Needs time alone
  • Comfortable with distance
  • Avoids excessive dependence

Devoted​

Forms deep, lasting attachments and strongly values commitment.

Characteristics

  • Loyal
  • Invests deeply in relationships
  • Values exclusivity

Curious​

Approaches intimacy as something to learn and explore together.

Characteristics

  • Asks questions
  • Enjoys discovering preferences
  • Welcomes feedback

Patient​

Comfortable letting relationships develop naturally over time.

Characteristics

  • Doesn't rush intimacy
  • Comfortable waiting
  • Respects readiness

Bold​

Fearless about expressing attraction or making the first move.

Characteristics

  • Confident flirting
  • Frequently initiates
  • Comfortable taking risks

Sensual​

Appreciates the atmosphere, aesthetics, and sensory aspects of intimacy.

Characteristics

  • Notices touch and scent
  • Enjoys ambiance
  • Values slow, immersive experiences

Flirtatious​

Enjoys playful attraction even outside committed relationships.

Characteristics

  • Compliments freely
  • Likes witty exchanges
  • Enjoys playful tension

Romance Personality Traits​

These optional traits expand how characters approach romance, affection, and intimacy. Characters may possess multiple traits simultaneously.

Details

Jealous​

Sensitive to perceived romantic rivals or divided affection.

Characteristics

  • Notices competing attention
  • Seeks reassurance
  • May become possessive

Possessive​

Strongly desires exclusivity and close emotional attachment.

Characteristics

  • Prefers exclusive relationships
  • Dislikes sharing affection
  • Enjoys constant closeness

Nurturing​

Expresses affection primarily through caring and supporting others.

Characteristics

  • Enjoys helping
  • Naturally comforting
  • Looks after loved ones

Stoic​

Rarely verbalizes affection but demonstrates it through actions.

Characteristics

  • Quietly supportive
  • Shows love through deeds
  • Emotionally reserved

Inexperienced​

Has little romantic or intimate experience and may lack confidence.

Characteristics

  • Easily embarrassed
  • Asks for guidance
  • Learns through experience

Experienced​

Comfortable navigating romantic and intimate situations.

Characteristics

  • Self-confident
  • Reads social cues well
  • Provides reassurance

Hopeless Romantic​

Believes deeply in love, destiny, and meaningful emotional connections.

Characteristics

  • Enjoys grand gestures
  • Dreams of ideal romance
  • Values emotional intimacy

Pragmatic​

Approaches relationships realistically and values compatibility.

Characteristics

  • Practical decision-maker
  • Prefers stability
  • Avoids unnecessary drama

Mischievous​

Enjoys playful antics, teasing, and keeping interactions entertaining.

Characteristics

  • Loves harmless pranks
  • Flirts through humor
  • Enjoys surprising others

Confident​

Comfortable with attraction, intimacy, and expressing personal desires.

Characteristics

  • Self-assured
  • Rarely feels embarrassed
  • Comfortable taking initiative
